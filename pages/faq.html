Frequently Asked Questions

What is CurioCrow?
CurioCrow is a student-built platform for the IMY320 module at the University of Pretoria. It allows users to explore, catalogue, and manage coin collections in an interactive way, demonstrating how multimedia design and web technologies can be applied in real-world contexts.
What is IMY320 about?
IMY320 is a multimedia module focusing on the use of technology and design in modern applications. It explores topics like UX principles, design laws, marketing concepts, and emerging technologies. The course encourages critical thinking, ethical AI use, and a balance between creativity and technical skill.
Can I add real personal data on this site?
No. This project is for academic purposes only — please avoid entering any personal, sensitive, or confidential information. The University and developers are not responsible for data loss or leakage resulting from such input.
How does this project relate to the IMY320 module content?
CurioCrow integrates themes from IMY320 by combining multimedia design, interactivity, and web development. It demonstrates applied UX design, interface logic, and responsible AI-assisted creation — aligning with the module’s emphasis on understanding technology’s practical and ethical implications.
What are some tips for starting a coin collection?
Start with coins that interest you — such as a specific country, theme, or era. Learn to identify mint marks, materials, and conditions. Store coins properly in acid-free holders and avoid touching them directly to prevent oxidation. Join local or online communities to trade and expand your collection safely.
How should I verify a coin’s authenticity?
Examine the coin’s weight, dimensions, and details against official mint data. Use a magnifying glass or digital microscope to look for inconsistencies. When in doubt, consult a certified coin appraiser or numismatist before purchasing or trading.
